## 3. ANIMATION STATE MACHINE

```
ANIMATION STATE MACHINE
?????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

INITIAL STATE
?
??? setCurrentAnimation(0)
?   ?
?   ??? Select which animation (0-10)
?   ??? Load its texture
?   ??? Reset frame counter
?   ??? Restart animation clock

PLAYING
?
??? Call nextFrame() every game frame
?   ?
?   ??? Check: Has enough time passed?
?   ?   ?? if (clock.elapsed >= frameTimer)
?   ?
?   ??? If YES:
?   ?   ?? Restart clock
?   ?   ?? Increment currentFrame
?   ?   ?   ?? 0 ? 1 ? 2 ? ... ? (frames-1)
?   ?   ?
?   ?   ?? Check: Animation complete?
?   ?   ?? if (currentFrame >= frames)
?   ?           ?
?   ?           ??? Increment cycles counter
?   ?       ??? Check: Enough cycles?
?   ?   ?   ?? if (cycles >= AnimationForCycles)
?   ?           ?       ?
?   ?           ?  ??? YES: Animation finished
?   ?     ?       ?   ?? Reset to animation 0
?   ?           ? ?   ?? Call afterAnimation callback
?   ?           ?       ?
?   ?           ?       ??? NO: Keep playing
?   ?           ?
?   ?           ?? Wrap frame: currentFrame %= frames
?   ? ?? (frames-1) ? 0 (loop)
?   ?
?   ??? If NO:
?       ?? Wait until time passes
?
??? Use currentFrame for texture cropping

TEXTURE CROPPING CALCULATION
?
??? frameWidth = textureWidth / totalFrames
?
??? crop rectangle:
?   ?? X = currentFrame * frameWidth
?   ?? Y = 0
?   ?? Width = frameWidth
?   ?? Height = textureHeight
?
??? Display only this portion of texture

ANIMATION CHAINING
?
??? Main animation completes
? ?
?   ??? Calls afterAnimation callback
?
??? Callback can start new animation:
?   ?
?   ??? DoAnimation(cycles, index)
?   ?   ??? Set AnimationForCycles = cycles
??   ??? Switch to animation[index]
? ?   ??? Restart clocks
?   ?
?   ??? New animation begins (go to PLAYING)
?
??? Chain multiple animations:
    Attack ? Digest ? Idle ? (repeat)
```

```

?????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

?
??? isUsingAbility = true
??? abilityClock is running
?
??? Every frame:
?   ?
?   ??? Execute ability behavior:
?   ?   ?? Ra: Pull suns toward self
?   ?   ?? Healing plant: Heal nearby plants
?   ?   ?? Freeze: Apply freeze effect

?   ?? Clear ability flags
```

## 8. GRID & POSITIONING SYSTEM

```
GRID LAYOUT
?????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

Screen (1920 x 1080):
  ??????????????????????????????????????????????????????
  ?     UI Section (100px)          ?
  ?     Money, buttons, etc.   ?
  ??????????????????????????????????????????????????????
  ?             ?
  ?  [×××]  ? Grid Rows = 5           ?
  ?  [×××]        Row 0: offsetY + (0 * 170)          ?
  ?  [×××]        Row 1: offsetY + (1 * 170)     ?
  ?  [×××]        Row 2: offsetY + (2 * 170)          ?
  ?  [×××]     Row 3: offsetY + (3 * 170)   ?
  ?              Row 4: offsetY + (4 * 170) ?
  ?         ?
  ?  offsetX      Grid Cols = 9         ?
  ?  (470px)      Col 0: offsetX + (0 * 150)          ?
  ?         Col 1: offsetX + (1 * 150)          ?
  ?  ...  ?
  ?        Col 8: offsetX + (8 * 150)          ?
  ?      ?
  ??????????????????????????????????????????????????????

COORDINATE CONVERSION:

World Position ? Grid Cell:
  ??? column = (x - offsetX) / cellWidth
  ??? row = (y - offsetY) / cellHeight
  ??? gridCell = {row, column}

Grid Cell ? World Position:
  ??? x = offsetX + (col * cellWidth) + centerOffset
  ??? y = offsetY + (row * cellHeight) + bottomOffset
  ??? position = {x, y}

Zombie starting position:
  ??? x = windowWidth (far right, will move left)
  ??? y = offsetY + (randomRow * cellHeight)
  ??? Starts at grid edge, moves toward left

Plant placement:
  ??? x = offsetX + (col * cellWidth) + (cellWidth - width) / 2
  ?     (Center horizontally)
  ??? y = offsetY + (row * cellHeight) + (cellHeight - height)
  ?  (Align to bottom)
  ??? Fixed position (doesn't move)
```
